✨ QUICK REVIEWER: Tense of a Verb

📝 TENSE OF A VERB – tells when an action happens.

🟢 SIMPLE PRESENT

An action that happens regularly, repeatedly, or is a fact.

✨ Examples:
👧 She reads every night.
👦 They play after class.

🔑 Common clues: always, usually, often, sometimes, every day, every week

⚠️ Rule: For he, she, it, usually add -s/-es.

  • 👉 study → studies
  • 👉 watch → watches
  • 👉 play → plays

🔵 SIMPLE PAST

An action that already happened or was completed.

✨ Examples:
📖 She studied last night.
🏫 We visited the library yesterday.

🔑 Common clues: yesterday, last night, last week, ago, earlier, before

✏️ Regular verbs: usually add -ed:
👉 walk → walked
👉 clean → cleaned

⚠️ Irregular verbs:
👉 go → went
👉 eat → ate
👉 see → saw
👉 write → wrote
👉 take → took

🟣 SIMPLE FUTURE

An action that will happen in the future.

📌 Rule: will + base form of the verb

✨ Examples:
📚 I will study tonight.
🏫 They will visit the museum tomorrow.

🔑 Common clues: tomorrow, later, soon, next week, next month, next year

⚠️ Remember: After WILL, always use the BASE FORM.
✅ She will study. (❌ She will studies.)
✅ He will play. (❌ He will played.)

🧠 QUICK GUIDE

  • 🟢 PRESENT → happens NOW / REGULARLY
  • 🔵 PAST → happened BEFORE
  • 🟣 FUTURE → will happen LATER

📌 Example:
🟢 I walk to school every day.
🔵 I walked to school yesterday.
🟣 I will walk to school tomorrow.

⭐ REMEMBER! 👀 Look for time clues. ✏️ Change the verb correctly. 📝 Make sure the sentence still makes sense.

🏆 PRESENT = NOW/REGULAR
🏆 PAST = BEFORE
🏆 FUTURE = WILL HAPPEN
