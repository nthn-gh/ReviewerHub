✨ QUICK REVIEWER: Context Clues

🔎 CONTEXT CLUES – words, phrases, or sentences around an unfamiliar word that help you figure out its meaning.

🧠 Remember: 👀 LOOK → 💭 THINK → 💡 INFER → ✅ CHECK

📌 TYPES OF CONTEXT CLUES

  • 📖 DENOTATION – the literal or dictionary meaning of a word.
  • 💭 CONNOTATION – the feeling, idea, or association connected to a word.
  • 🔗 ANALOGY – compares two things with a similar relationship.
    Example: Teacher : Student :: Coach : Athlete
  • 👤 APPOSITIVE – a noun or phrase beside another noun that gives more information about it.
    Example: Maria, a kind learner, helped her classmates.
  • 🧩 GENERAL GIST/SENSE – use the overall meaning of the sentence or passage.
  • ✏️ PUNCTUATION – commas, dashes, parentheses, and colons may provide explanations or additional information.

📖 WORDS TO REVIEW

  • 🐘 Enormous – very big
  • 🙏 Grateful – thankful
  • 💧 Slippery – wet or smooth and difficult to walk or hold
  • ⚠️ Cautious – careful to avoid danger
  • ⏱️ Brief – short
  • 🐶 Playful – fond of playing
  • 🥛 Fragile – easily broken
  • ❓ Curious – wanting to know or learn something
  • 🤝 Generous – willing to give or share
  • 🤫 Silent – quiet; making no sound

🌟 TEST TIP: Read the whole sentence, find the clues, think about the meaning, and choose an answer that makes sense in the sentence.
